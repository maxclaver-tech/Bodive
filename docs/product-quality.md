# Product quality gate

Private review only. All budget category totals, monthly dashboard references, annual totals, blank inputs and numeric validation are checked structurally. English and Spanish variants preserve internal worksheet names. Cover title fits its available width. Review packages include a quality report and checksums. Automated tests do not evaluate Excel formulas or certify visual quality; a human must open the workbook, recalculate and inspect both languages before separate publication approval. No external publication or paid generation.
